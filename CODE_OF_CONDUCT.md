# Code of Conduct

ligungi is a place to play, learn and build Gungi together. We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or the language they speak.

This code applies to the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews and commit comments), to the [Discord server](https://ligungi.org/discord) and to anyone speaking for ligungi elsewhere. The Discord server also has its own channel rules, which follow this code. Play on ligungi.org is covered by the site's [terms](https://ligungi.org/terms).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to Gungi, to programming or to the language you are writing in.
- Disagree about moves, code and ideas, not about people.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Think about what is best for the community, not only for you.

## What is not acceptable

- Slurs, insults, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind.
- Harassment, in public or in private, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Sexual, gory or shocking content: the community is open to all ages.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Cheating, or helping someone cheat, in games against people.
- Spam, and promoting other projects or products where they were not asked for.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a friendly community.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **contato@ligungi.org**. On Discord you can also use Report Message or mention @Moderator. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private, and nobody will be punished for a report made in good faith.

Security vulnerabilities are not reported here: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers and moderators decide what fits this code and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and messages that do not. Depending on what happened, they may:

1. **Correct:** a private note explaining what was wrong, and maybe a request for a public apology.
2. **Warn:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and a period without contact with the people involved.
3. **Suspend:** a temporary ban from interacting with the community.
4. **Ban:** a permanent ban, for a pattern of violations or a serious one, such as harassment or threats.

To contest a decision, write to contato@ligungi.org.
