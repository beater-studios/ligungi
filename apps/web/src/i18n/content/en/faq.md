## What is Gungi?

A two-player strategy game created for the HUNTER×HUNTER manga and released as a real game in 2022. It resembles shogi, but pieces can be stacked into towers of up to three, and the height of the tower extends the reach of the piece on top. See [how to play](/learn).

## Are these really the official rules?

Yes. We follow the 2022 rulebook and the official Universal Music FAQ. The sources are on the [About](/about) page, and every ambiguous point, with the interpretation we chose, is in the [rules specification](spec).

## Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free, with no ads. You can play without an account; with one you play rated games, join the leaderboard, follow friends and keep your history on your profile.

## Is there an app?

ligungi works in the browser on any device, and the browser can also install it as an app: on a phone, use Add to Home Screen (or Install app); on a computer, the install icon in the address bar. On Windows it is also in the [Microsoft Store](microsoftStore). The app opens the same site, with your account, settings and games, and once installed the computer, the lessons, the analysis board and the rules also work without a connection.

## Can I play with a screen reader?

Yes. Turn on screen reader mode in Settings or at the top of the sign in page. In games, every move is announced in words, and a field takes typed moves (for example, 6-6 samurai) and questions about the position, the hands, a square or the clocks, and there is a board you can move through with the arrow keys. It works on every screen with a board: games, TV, the analysis board, studies, the editor, lessons, puzzles, practice and the coordinate trainer. The board stays on screen for anyone helping.

## How does the rating work?

We use Glicko-2, with a separate rating for each mode. While a rating is still uncertain it shows a "?" and the player is not on the leaderboard. Casual games do not change ratings.

## Why can I not make a move that puts my Marshal in danger?

By the rulebook, whoever lets their Marshal be captured loses. Instead of allowing the move and waiting for the capture, the site simply does not offer it. If you have no move that keeps your Marshal safe, the game ends in mate, even if it is not under attack at that moment.

## What are the draft and "済み"?

In the Intermediate and Advanced modes, each player builds their own army by placing pieces in their territory, one at a time, starting with the Marshal. "済み" means "done": a player who declares it places no more pieces. By the rulebook, when the second player declares done the draft ends for both. Casual games offer a "free draft" option where each player finishes their own.

## What is FEN?

A line of text that describes a whole position: the towers on the board, the pieces in hand, whose turn it is, the mode and the phase. On the analysis board you can copy and paste positions with it.

## Is ligungi connected to HUNTER×HUNTER or Universal Music?

No. It is a fan project, not officially affiliated.

## Where can I talk with other players?

On our [Discord server](discord), also at discord.ligungi.org: find opponents, share games, talk strategy and ask about the rules. It has channels in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

## I found a bug. How do I report it?

Post it in #feedback on our [Discord server](discord), open an [issue on GitHub](issues) or write to {email}. For security issues, use the [private report](security), never a public issue.

## Can I help?

Yes. The code is open and welcomes contributions: rule corrections backed by a source, translations, code and bug reports. Start at the [repository](repo), or say hello in #development or #translations on our [Discord server](discord).
