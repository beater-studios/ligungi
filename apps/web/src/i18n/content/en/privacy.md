This policy explains what data ligungi.org processes and why, in line with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD). The data controller is [Beater Studios](studio). Contact: {email}.

## Summary

- No ads, no trackers and no third-party cookies.
- We only keep what the game needs to work.
- Your data is not sold or shared for marketing.

## What we process

- **Account (if you create one):** username and password. The password is only stored as a cryptographic hash, which not even we can read. If you turn on two-step verification, we also store the key that generates the codes of your authenticator app and the recovery codes, these only as hashes. All of it is deleted when you turn the verification off or delete your account (an unfinished setup is deleted when cancelled or after 30 minutes), and your data download only says whether it is on.
- **Games and activity:** moves, results, ratings, players you follow and your account creation date. This data appears on your public profile. Who may challenge you and who may follow you are saved with your account, and so are the notices of the bell (who started following you and what the moderators decided about your account or your reports), up to 50, deleted with the account. Challenges for live games are kept only in the server's memory while they wait for an answer. Correspondence challenges and open games, which wait up to 7 days, are stored in the database so a server restart does not lose them: open games are deleted when they expire and challenges 14 days after they were made, or sooner if you delete your account, and they are part of your data download.
- **Session cookie (sid):** an essential cookie that identifies your browser to keep you signed in and keep your games. It is not used for tracking. Sessions without an account expire after 30 days unused; with an account, after a year. With each signed-in session we also keep a description of the browser (the "user agent", for example "Chrome on Windows") and when it signed in and was last used, so you can see on the Account page where your account is open.
- **Moderation:** reports (who reported whom, the reason, the text and the game or the study) are kept for the moderators to look into, and are deleted when either account is deleted. Moderator actions (suspensions, name changes, voided games and hidden or deleted studies) go to a log with the moderator, the date and the reason. Your data download includes the reports you made and the actions on your account.
- **IP address:** used at request time to limit login attempts and protect the site from abuse. It is not stored in our database.
- **Visit statistics:** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts, in aggregate, the pages visited, the country, the type of browser and the loading time. It uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not identify you. It is only turned on if you accept it in the cookie notice, and you can change your choice at the bottom of this page.
- **Profile:** the bio, the country and the usernames on other sites that you choose to fill in on the Account page. They show on your public profile, are part of your data copy and are deleted with the account. Moderators may delete a bio and links that break the terms of use.
- **Lessons, puzzles and practice:** the stars of each lesson exercise, your puzzle rating and your tries, with the date and the result, your Puzzle Streak and Puzzle Storm results, the practice positions you finished, and the games you bookmarked with your private notes, kept on the account so your progress shows on any device. Without an account they stay in your browser only. They are part of your data copy and are deleted with the account.
- **Studies:** the studies you create, with their title, description, visibility, chapters, moves, comments and drawings. A public study shows in the study list and an unlisted one opens for anyone with the link; a private one opens only for you, not even for the moderators. They are part of your data copy and are deleted with the account.
- **Discord link (optional):** if you link your account to Discord on the Account page, we store your Discord id and username, the access tokens Discord gives us (encrypted) and your current rank. We send Discord your ligungi username, your best established rating, your number of rated games and your account creation date, so it can show the connection on your Discord profile and give you the matching roles on our server. The tokens only allow reading who you are on Discord and writing those fields: they give no access to your messages, servers or email. Unlinking or deleting your account removes the roles and deletes the link at once; if you revoke ligungi on Discord instead, that happens the next time we update your data there. The link is part of your data copy, never the tokens.
- **Preferences:** language, theme, sound and board size stay in your browser (local storage) and are not sent to us.

## Why we process it (legal bases)

- Account, games and session: to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract).
- IP and protections: legitimate interest in keeping the site safe and running.
- Reports and moderation: legitimate interest in keeping games fair and the site free of abuse, as the terms of use say.
- Visit statistics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- Discord link: your consent, given when you link the account, which you can withdraw by unlinking it.

## Who we share it with

The site's traffic goes through Cloudflare, which provides a content delivery network (CDN), protection against attacks and the visit statistics, and may process technical data such as IP addresses, including outside Brazil (see [Cloudflare's policy](cloudflarePrivacy)). The site runs on a hosting provider contracted by Beater Studios. Discord receives the fields described above only if you link your account to it (see [Discord's privacy policy](discordPrivacy)). Otherwise, we only share data when the law requires it.

## How long

- Account and games: while the account exists. Games are part of the site's public history.
- Sessions: until they expire, as described above.
- Backups: up to 14 days.
- Moderation log: kept even after an account is deleted, as the record of the decisions taken.

## Your rights

Under the LGPD you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and information about how it is processed. On the [Account](/account) page you can download a copy of your data, sign out other devices, change your password and delete your account yourself; for anything else, write to {email} with the subject "Privacy", including your username. Deleting your account removes your personal data; games already played may remain in the history anonymously.
