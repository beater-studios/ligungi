ligungi's rules follow, in order of authority:

1. The official rulebook (軍儀解説書), released in 2022 by Universal Music with the physical board.
2. The [official Universal Music FAQ](rulebookFaq) (questions Q1 to Q30), which clarifies cases and fixes a misprint in the rulebook.
3. The [English translation of the rulebook](rulebookEnglish), checked page by page against the original.
4. [軍儀Web](gungiWeb), a Japanese site that follows the official rules, used only where the rulebook is silent.

Universal Music here means Universal Music Japan, the Japanese branch of the Universal Music Group record label, which also releases licensed anime and manga products. Gungi came out as an official licensed HUNTER×HUNTER product, that is, with the permission of the work's rights holders: the rules were written by a team dedicated to the game (HUNTER×HUNTER 軍儀 特別チーム), supervised by Shueisha, the publisher of Yoshihiro Togashi's manga.

Where the rulebook leaves room for doubt, the interpretation we adopted and why are in the [full rules specification](spec), in the project's source code.
