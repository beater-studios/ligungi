# API

The types of every response and message are in [packages/@ligungi/protocol/src](../packages/@ligungi/protocol/src), one file per area (games, lobby, players, account, moderation, socket messages, studies, puzzles and so on), all re-exported by `index.ts`.

The session is a `sid` cookie, created by `GET /api/me` or by the first write. Login, signup and logout replace it; anonymous games, seeks and challenges move to the new session, and WebSocket connections opened with the old one are closed with code `4001` so the client reconnects. Writes (`POST`, `DELETE`) need `Content-Type: application/json` (send `{}` even with an empty body) and cannot come from another origin.

## Notation

- Moves: `e3e4` (move), `e3e4=` (stack), `e3e4x` (capture), `Y*e3` (drop from the hand, piece letter in uppercase), `^` plus levels for betrayal (`e3e4=^12`), `done` to declare done in the draft. Squares `a1` to `i9`, with `a1` in White's left corner.
- Piece letters: M Marshal, G General, L Lieutenant General, J Major General, S Samurai, N Lancer, R Knight, Y Ninja, F Fortress, P Soldier, C Cannon, A Archer, K Musketeer, T Tactician. Uppercase for White, lowercase for Black.
- FEN: `<board> <turn> <hands> <mode> <phase> <first>`, described in `packages/@ligungi/engine/src/fen.ts`. A `+` at the end of the phase (`d+`, `p+`) means free draft.

## HTTP

| Method and path | Description |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/version` | `{ version, commit }`: the project version and the git commit of the running build. |
| `GET /api/health` | `{ ok: true, version, commit }` after a database query succeeds, `503` otherwise. Meant for uptime monitors. |
| `GET /api/me` | The session's user, their ongoing games, whether they moderate the site and their puzzle rating (`null` without an account). |
| `POST /api/signup` `{ username, password }` | Creates an account and signs in. |
| `POST /api/login` `{ username, password, code? }` | Signs in. A suspended account gets `403 accountSuspended` with `until` (epoch ms, `null` for a permanent suspension) and `reason`. For an account with two-step verification on, a request without `code` answers `401 twoFactorRequired` after checking the password; the client sends the password again with `code`, a 6-digit code from the authenticator app or a recovery code (`401 wrongTwoFactorCode` when wrong or already used). Wrong codes count as failed logins of the account. |
| `POST /api/logout` | Signs out. |
| `POST /api/account/password` `{ current, next }` | Changes the password (`wrongPassword` when `current` does not match). Signs out every other session and rotates the current one. |
| `POST /api/account/delete` `{ password }` | Deletes the account: ratings, follows, blocks and sessions are removed, seeks and challenges cancelled, and finished games keep the moves with the player shown as `Anonymous`. Refused with `409 activeGame` while the player has an ongoing game. Returns `{ user: null }`. |
| `GET /api/users/:name/stats/:mode` | A player's rated games in one mode (`ModeStats`): the rating after each game (oldest first, at most 2000, rating before plus the change as stored), the peak and the lowest with their dates, and the results by color and by length (up to 60 moves, 61 to 120, over 120, counting only the moves after the draft). Voided games are left out. |
| `POST /api/reports` `{ username, reason, details, gameId? }` or `{ studyId, chapterId?, chapterPath?, reason, details }` | Reports a player, or one of their studies, to the moderators (signed in): `reason` is `cheating`, `name`, `behavior`, `content` (what a study shows) or `other`, `details` up to 500 characters. A study report can name the chapter and the move the reporter was on (`chapterPath`, the move's path in the chapter's tree, as `0.1.0`); a chapter of another study is dropped, and so is a path that is not one. One open report per reporter and player, counting reports about each study and each of its chapters apart (`409 alreadyReported`), at most 10 per hour. A report about a study carries `studyId`, `chapterId` and `chapterPath` in `ReportView`, and the moderators' link opens the study there (`/study/<id>?chapter=<chapter>&move=<path>`). |
| `GET /api/mod/reports?status=open\|closed` | Moderators: the reports (`ReportView[]`), open ones oldest first, closed ones latest first. Anyone else gets `403 notModerator`. |
| `POST /api/mod/reports/:id/close` `{ resolution }` | Moderators: closes a report with a note. |
| `GET /api/mod/log` | Moderators: the latest 100 entries of the moderation log (`ModLogEntry[]`). |
| `GET /api/mod/users/:name` | Moderators: a player's standing (`ModUserView`): moderator or not, the suspension in force, open reports about them and their log. |
| `POST /api/mod/users/:name/suspend` `{ days, reason }` | Moderators: suspends for 1, 3, 7 or 30 days, or for good with `days: null`. Ends the player's sessions, cancels their seeks and challenges and loses their games in progress. Not on moderators (`403 cannotModerateModerator`). |
| `POST /api/mod/users/:name/unsuspend` `{ reason }` | Moderators: lifts a suspension (`409 notSuspended` without one). |
| `POST /api/mod/users/:name/rename` `{ username, reason }` | Moderators: a new username, also in the stored games, challenges and seeks. |
| `POST /api/mod/users/:name/clear-profile` `{ reason }` | Moderators: deletes the bio and the linked profiles of a player; the country stays. |
| `POST /api/mod/studies/:id/hide` `{ hidden?, reason }` | Moderators: hides a study (`hidden` defaults to `true`) or shows it again with `hidden: false`. A hidden study is left out of the list and opens only for its author and the moderators. Logged as `hideStudy` or `unhideStudy`. |
| `POST /api/mod/studies/:id/chapters/:chapterId/clear-drawings` `{ reason }` | Moderators: removes every arrow and painted square of a chapter, keeping its moves and comments. The author gets a notice (`studyDrawingsCleared`); logged as `clearDrawings`. |
| `POST /api/mod/studies/:id/delete` `{ reason }` | Moderators: deletes a study with its chapters. Logged as `deleteStudy`. |
| `POST /api/mod/games/:id/void` `{ reason }` | Moderators: voids a finished rated game, undoing both players' rating changes from it (`409 gameNotVoidable` otherwise). `GameView` and `GameSummary` carry `voided`. |
| `GET /api/account/two-factor` | Two-step verification status (`TwoFactorView`): whether it is on and how many recovery codes are left. |
| `POST /api/account/two-factor/setup` `{ password }` | Starts the setup and returns `TwoFactorSetup`: a new secret in base32 and the `otpauth://` link for the QR code. Nothing changes until it is confirmed; `409 twoFactorEnabled` when already on. |
| `DELETE /api/account/two-factor/setup` | Drops a setup that was not confirmed (the site calls it on Cancel or when leaving the page). An unconfirmed setup also expires after 30 minutes. |
| `POST /api/account/two-factor/enable` `{ code, signOutOthers? }` | Confirms the setup with a first code from the app and turns it on; with `signOutOthers: true` it also signs out every other session of the account. Returns `{ recoveryCodes, revoked }`: ten single-use codes, shown only this once, and how many sessions were signed out (`409 twoFactorNotSetUp` without a setup started). |
| `POST /api/account/two-factor/recovery` `{ code }` | Replaces the recovery codes, after a code from the app (or a recovery code). Returns `{ recoveryCodes }`. |
| `POST /api/account/two-factor/disable` `{ password, code }` | Turns it off, after the password and a code from the app or a recovery code. |
| `GET /api/account/sessions` | The account's signed-in sessions (`SessionView`), most recently active first: a public handle (never the session secret), whether it is the current one, sign-in and last-use times (to the day) and the browser's user agent. |
| `DELETE /api/account/sessions` | Signs out every other session of the account and closes their sockets. Returns `{ revoked }`. |
| `DELETE /api/account/sessions/:id` | Signs out one other session by its handle (`sessionNotFound` for an unknown handle or the current session). |
| `GET /api/account/profile` | The player's own `ProfileDetails`: `{ bio, country, links }`. |
| `POST /api/account/profile` `{ bio, country, links }` | Saves the profile. `bio`: plain text up to 400 characters (line breaks kept, at most one blank line in a row). `country`: an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code in lowercase (`COUNTRIES`) or `null`. `links`: the player's name on each site of `PROFILE_LINKS` (social networks and the lila-based game sites), never an address: the site builds `https://<prefix><name>`. A pasted address of the same site, a leading `@` and a trailing slash are accepted and reduced to the name; anything else that does not match the site's pattern is refused (`invalidProfileLink`), as are a long bio (`bioTooLong`) and an unknown country (`invalidCountry`). Answers with the profile as stored. |
| `GET /api/lessons/progress` | The best stars (1 to 3) the player earned in each lesson exercise they finished, by exercise id. |
| `POST /api/lessons/progress` `{ results }` | Saves up to 100 lesson results, `{ <exercise id>: stars }` (ids from `LESSON_EXERCISES`, stars from 1 to 3; anything else refuses the request with `invalidLessonProgress`). Each exercise keeps its best stars. Answers with the whole progress. The site sends the results earned without an account the first time a lesson page loads signed in. |
| `GET /api/practice/progress` | The practice positions the player finished, with the fewest moves each took, by position id. |
| `POST /api/practice/progress` `{ results }` | Saves up to 100 finished positions, `{ <position id>: moves }` (ids from `PRACTICE_POSITIONS`, moves a whole number from 1 to 1000; anything else refuses the request with `invalidPracticeProgress`). Each position keeps its fewest moves. Answers with the whole progress. The site sends the positions finished without an account the first time a practice page loads signed in. |
| `GET /api/puzzles/next?theme=&mode=&exclude=` | A puzzle to try next (`{ id }`): one of the 12 whose rating is closest to the player's puzzle rating (1200 without an account), among those of the theme (`PUZZLE_THEMES`) and mode asked and not tried yet. Without an account, `exclude` lists the ids the browser already solved. `noPuzzleLeft` (404) when none is left. |
| `GET /api/puzzles/daily` | The puzzle of the day (`DailyPuzzle`: id, day in UTC, position, solving side, kind and length), the same for everyone, among the puzzles starting between 1000 and 1700. |
| `GET /api/puzzles/sequence?kind=` | The puzzles of a new Puzzle Streak or Puzzle Storm run (`kind`: `streak` or `storm`, `invalidPuzzleRun` otherwise), as `{ ids }` in order. Every mode is mixed, and the target rating rises along the run (from 1000, by 20 per puzzle in Streak and by 10 in Storm), each puzzle drawn among the 6 closest to it not used yet. Streak gets every puzzle, Storm 150. |
| `GET /api/racer` | `RacerLobbyView`: the player's Puzzle Racer scores of the last 8 days and best ever (`records`, null without an account), and how many players wait in the open public race (`waiting`). |
| `POST /api/racer/public` | Joins the open public race, or opens one, and returns `{ id }`. A public race starts 15 seconds after its first player joins (`RACE_PUBLIC_WAIT_MS`), or 5 seconds after it reaches 10 players. Up to 30 races created or joined per minute per session. |
| `POST /api/racer` | Creates a race to share by link (`kind: 'friends'`) and returns `{ id }`. It waits until its creator starts it, and is dropped after 30 minutes if nobody does. |
| `GET /api/racer/:id` | `RaceView`: the race's state, times as milliseconds from now (`startsIn`, `endsIn`), its puzzles once the start is set, the players with their scores, the ghosts, and the viewer's own progress (`you`). `raceNotFound` (404) for an unknown race; finished races are kept 10 minutes. |
| `POST /api/racer/:id/join` | Body `{}`, or `{ instanceId }` for a Discord call's race (which checks the caller is in that call). `raceStarted` (409) once it runs, `raceFull` (409) at 10 players, `notInActivity` (403) for a call race from outside the call. |
| `GET /api/arena` | `ArenaList`: the weekly Arena running, else the next one (`current`, an `ArenaView`), and the latest 10 finished or canceled ones (`recent`, `ArenaSummary`). |
| `GET /api/arena/:id` | `ArenaView`: status (`created`, `started`, `finished`, `canceled`), mode, clock, length, planned and real start, end, season, the standings (`ArenaStanding`: score, games, wins, performance, the score sheet with the doubled wins, the streak, the live state and, once finished, the trophy), the Arena games in progress and the viewer's own state (`you`). `arenaNotFound` (404). |
| `POST /api/arena/:id/join` | Joins the Arena, or comes back after a pause. Needs an account (`loginRequired`); `arenaOver` (409) once finished or canceled. Up to 30 joins and pauses per minute per session. Returns the `ArenaView`. |
| `POST /api/arena/:id/pause` | Before the start, leaves the Arena; while it runs, pauses the player (no new pairing until they join again). Returns the `ArenaView`. |
| `GET /api/users/:name/arenas` | `PlayerArenas`: the player's trophy counts and Arenas played, their latest 8 trophies (each with its place, the number of players, the season and the date) and their latest 10 Arenas. |
| `POST /api/racer/:id/start` | Starts a private race in 5 seconds (`RACE_COUNTDOWN_MS`). Only its creator: `notRaceOwner` (409) otherwise. |
| `GET /api/puzzles/runs` | `PuzzleRunsView`: the player's runs of the last 8 days (enough for the best of today and of the week in any time zone) and the best score ever of each kind. |
| `POST /api/puzzles/runs` `{ runs }` | Saves up to 100 finished runs, each `{ kind, score, at }` (score a whole number from 0 to the number of puzzles, `at` in ms and not in the future; anything else refuses the request with `invalidPuzzleRun`). Answers with `PuzzleRunsView`. The site sends each run as it ends, and the runs played without an account the first time a run page loads signed in. Runs do not touch the puzzle rating. |
| `GET /api/puzzles/:id` | `PuzzleView`: the puzzle's live rating, how many first tries it had, and the viewer's own first try. |
| `POST /api/puzzles/:id/attempt` `{ win }` | Records the player's try. Only the first try at a puzzle counts: it is scored as a game between the player's puzzle rating and the puzzle's (Glicko-2), and both change. Answers with `PuzzleAttemptResult` (`counted: false` and no change for a later try). The site sends a win when the puzzle was solved without a wrong move or a look at the solution, and a loss on the first wrong move or when the solution is shown. |
| `GET /api/puzzles/dashboard` | `PuzzleDashboard`: the puzzle rating, every counted try with the rating after it, and tries and wins by theme. |
| `GET /api/puzzles/solved` / `POST /api/puzzles/solved` `{ ids }` | The puzzles the player solved. The POST brings up to 200 puzzles solved without an account into it (unknown ids refuse the request with `invalidPuzzle`): they count as solved, without touching any rating. |
| `GET /api/explorer?mode=&band=&rated=1&fen=` | Draft explorer (`ExplorerView`): for a draft position (`fen`, the mode's empty start when left out), how many finished games reached it and with what results, and every next placement or "done" played from it, each with its games, White wins, draws, Black wins and the average rating of both players. `mode` is `intermediate` or `advanced` (`invalidMode` otherwise). `band` (`EXPLORER_BANDS`) keeps games whose players' average rating falls in the range, both players rated; `rated=1` keeps rated games only. A bad band, a FEN that does not parse, is out of the draft or belongs to another mode gives `invalidExplorerQuery`. Voided games, free draft games and games where Black moved first (from before White always started) are left out. The figures are rebuilt from the stored games at most every 10 minutes per filter, and `updatedAt` says when. |
| `GET /api/account/settings` | Account settings kept on the server: `{ challengePolicy, followPolicy }`. |
| `POST /api/account/settings` `{ challengePolicy?, followPolicy? }` | Either or both. `challengePolicy`: who may challenge the player from their profile, `everyone` (any signed-in player), `following` (only players they follow) or `nobody` (`invalidChallengePolicy` otherwise); challenge links are not affected. `followPolicy`: who may start following the player, with the same three values (`invalidFollowPolicy` otherwise); the followers they already have stay. Returns both settings. |
| `POST /api/notices/read` | Marks the account's notices as read and sends the `notices` message again to its sockets. |
| `GET /api/discord/link` | A page visit, not a fetch: sends the signed-in player to Discord's authorization page (scopes `identify` and `role_connections.write`) with a single-use state valid for 10 minutes; a signed-out visitor goes to `/login?next=/api/discord/link`. It is also the app's Linked Roles verification URL. Redirects to `/account?discord=disabled` when the server has no Discord settings. Up to 10 per minute per account. |
| `GET /api/discord/callback?code=&state=` | Where Discord sends the player back. Checks the state against the session's account, trades the code for tokens (stored encrypted), stores the link, sends the Linked Roles fields and gives the rank role, then redirects to `/account?discord=<result>` (`DISCORD_LINK_RESULTS`): `linked`, `denied` (the player cancelled on Discord), `failed`, `taken` (the Discord account is linked to another ligungi account) or `disabled`. |
| `GET /api/account/discord` | `DiscordLinkView`: whether the server has Discord settings (`enabled`) and the link, if any: Discord name, rank (`DISCORD_TIERS`, null while every rating is provisional), whether it is paused and when it was made. |
| `POST /api/account/discord/check` | For a paused link (the player left the Discord server): asks Discord once whether they are back; if so, removes the pause and sends everything again. Returns `DiscordLinkView`. `discordDisabled` or `discordNotLinked` (404); up to 10 per minute per account. |
| `DELETE /api/account/discord` | Unlinks: removes the rank role, clears the Linked Roles connection, revokes the authorization on Discord and deletes the link. Returns `DiscordLinkView`. |
| `GET /api/activity/config` | The Discord application id for the Activity, `{ clientId }`. `activityDisabled` (404) without the Discord settings. |
| `POST /api/activity/session` | Body `{ code }`, the authorization code the Activity got from Discord. Trades it for the player's Discord identity and starts a new session: the linked ligungi account, or a guest. Returns `{ user, reason, accessToken }`: `user` is null for a guest, `reason` says why (`unlinked`, `suspended` or `twoFactor`; null when signed in), and `accessToken` is Discord's token, for the SDK's `authenticate`. `activitySignInFailed` (400 or 401); up to 30 per minute per session. |
| `GET /api/activity/rooms?instanceId=` | The call's rooms and games, `ActivityHallView`: `rooms` (challenges waiting for a second player, oldest first, each `{ challengeId, settings, challenger, mine }`) and `games` (its games in progress, `GameSummary`). The Discord hub asks every few seconds. `activitySignInRequired` (401) without an Activity session; up to 120 per minute per session. |
| `POST /api/activity/rooms` | Body `{ instanceId, mode, timeControl, color }`. Opens a room in the call, closing the one the player had open there, and returns `{ challengeId }`. Always casual; `timeControl` is one of `ACTIVITY_TIME_CONTROLS` (3+2, 5+3, 10+5, 15+10), else `invalidInitialTime` (400). At most 20 rooms wait at once in a call (`tooManyChallenges`, 409). A room is closed with `DELETE /api/challenges/:id`. |
| `POST /api/activity/race` | Body `{ instanceId }`. The call's Puzzle Racer race: joins the one not started yet, or creates one (`kind: 'call'`, started by its creator). Returns `{ id }`. Same checks as the other `POST` routes of the Activity. `GET /api/activity/rooms` also returns it as `race` (`{ id, status, players }`, null when there is none). |
| `POST /api/activity/rooms/:id/join` | Body `{ instanceId }`. Joins a room of that call: the game starts and both players are sent to it (`redirect`), and the reply is `{ gameId }`. `challengeUnavailable` (409) for a room that is not open in that call. Both `POST` routes: `activitySignInRequired` (401) without an Activity session, `notInActivity` (403) when Discord says the player is not in that call, up to 30 per minute per session. |
| `GET /api/account/export` | Downloads the account's data as JSON (`Content-Disposition: attachment`): account, settings, whether two-step verification is on (never its secret or codes), ratings, the Discord link (Discord id and name, when it was made and whether it is paused; never the tokens), following, followers, the players blocked, the notices of the bell, sessions (without secrets), every game with its moves, stored correspondence seeks and challenges, the puzzle, lesson and practice progress (puzzle runs included), the bookmarked games with their notes, the Arena trophies, and the studies with their chapters. Up to 5 per minute per account. |
| `GET /api/users?q=<prefix>` | Up to 10 players whose username starts with `prefix` (2 to 20 characters, case-insensitive), alphabetical, each as `{ user, online }`. Any other query returns `[]`. |
| `GET /api/users/:name` | Profile: online status, rating and leaderboard rank per mode, counts, wins, draws and losses of the rated games per mode (`records`), followers, whether the requester follows the player, whether the requester blocked them (`blocked`), whether the requester may challenge them (`canChallenge`) or start following them (`canFollow`), the bio, country and linked profiles (`details`), ongoing and recent games. |
| `GET /api/users/:name/games` | Finished games, 20 per page, newest first. Optional query filters: `mode`, `result` (`win`, `loss`, `draw`, from the player's point of view), `rated` (`1` or `0`), `color` (`white` or `black`), `vs` (opponent username) and `before` (ms, for paging). An unknown value returns `invalidFilter`. |
| `GET /api/crosstable/:a/:b` | `Crosstable`: the record between two players, from `a`'s side: each one's wins, the draws and the latest 10 results (`CROSSTABLE_RECENT`), oldest first. Every finished game counts, rated or not, voided ones aside. `userNotFound` (404) for an unknown name, `invalidFilter` when both are the same player. |
| `GET /api/users/:name/counts` | The player's `GameCounts` (finished games, rated, wins, losses, draws) among the games the `mode`, `color` and `vs` filters leave, as on `/games`; `result` and `rated` are ignored. The profile's tabs use it while a filter is on. |
| `GET /api/users/:name/activity` | `ActivityView`: what the player did in the last 30 days, newest first (at most 1000 events): finished games from their side (result, end reason, rating change, opponent), public studies they created and the day they joined. The player alone also gets their puzzles, puzzle runs, improved lessons and finished practice positions (`mine: true`). |
| `GET /api/users/:name/insights/:mode` | `InsightsView`: the player's rated games of one mode (the latest 2000, voided ones left out) by the opponent's rating difference, how they ended (for wins, draws and losses), pace (clock, none, correspondence), average moves on the board by result, the five most played opponents and, in the draft modes, the pieces placed per game and the results by pieces still in hand when play starts. `invalidMode` for an unknown mode. |
| `GET /api/feed` | Signed in: `FeedItem[]`, what the players they follow did in the last 7 days (finished games and public studies), newest first, at most 40. |
| `POST /api/users/:name/follow` | Follows the player (requires an account, up to 400; `403 followRefused` when their follow setting does not allow it). Returns `{ followed, canFollow, followers, following }` of the followed player, who gets a `follow` notice the first time. |
| `DELETE /api/users/:name/follow` | Unfollows. Same answer; `canFollow` says whether following again would be allowed. |
| `POST /api/users/:name/block` | Blocks the player (requires an account, up to 400; `400 cannotBlockSelf`, `409 tooManyBlocks`). From then on neither can challenge or follow the other, they are never paired in the lobby, neither can take the other's open game, challenge link or rematch (`blockedPlayer`), the follows between them are removed and the open challenges between them canceled. The blocked player is not told. Returns `{ blocked, followed, canFollow, canChallenge, followers, following }`. |
| `DELETE /api/users/:name/block` | Unblocks. Same answer, with what is allowed again. |
| `GET /api/account/blocks` | The names of the players the account blocked, alphabetically. |
| `GET /api/following` | Players the account follows (`FriendView`): online status, ongoing game, ratings and whether the account may challenge them (their challenge setting). Requires an account. |
| `GET /api/players` | The players page (`PlayersView`): signed-in players online (at most 100, free ones first, then by best rating), each with their rating, rated games and leaderboard rank in every mode they played, their current game, whether the viewer follows them and may challenge them; counts (connected browsers, signed-in players online and how many are free, games in progress, games started in the last 24 hours, finished games, accounts); players with rated games per mode in bands of 50 points; the viewer's own ratings; and the recent activity (`recent`): the latest 8 finished games (`RECENT_GAMES`, voided ones aside) and the 6 newest accounts (`RECENT_PLAYERS`, banned ones aside), newest first. Counts, bands, ranks and the recent activity are recomputed at most once a minute. |
| `GET /api/leaderboard` | Top 10 of each mode. Here, on `/api/players` and on `/api/ranking`, every player also carries their `country` (or `null`). |
| `GET /api/leaderboard/:mode?limit=<n>` | Top 100 of one mode, or the first `limit` (1 to 100). Only players with an established rating are listed (deviation up to 110, out of the provisional period) who played a rated game in the mode in the last 30 days (`LEADERBOARD_ACTIVE_DAYS`); the same holds for `/api/ranking`, the ranks on profiles and the players page. Every entry, here and on `/api/ranking`, carries its `trend`: the rating points won or lost in the mode over the last 7 days (`RATING_TREND_DAYS`). |
| `GET /api/ranking/:mode?page=<n>&q=<text>&followed=1` | The ranking page (`RankingView`): one page of 50 rows of the mode's full leaderboard, each with position (ties share it), rating, rated games, wins, draws and losses, online status and whether the viewer follows the player. `q` keeps names containing the text and `followed=1` (signed in) keeps the viewer and the players they follow; `total` counts the matching rows. Also the number of ranked players per mode with the viewer's standing in each, and the viewer's position and page in this mode. Without `:mode`, the viewer's most played mode, otherwise the one with the most ranked players. |
| `GET /api/bookmarks` | The games the player bookmarked (`BookmarkedGame[]`: the game summary, the private note and when it was bookmarked), newest first, at most 200 (`BOOKMARKS_MAX`). Only the player sees them. |
| `GET /api/games/:id/bookmark` | `BookmarkView`: whether the player bookmarked the game, and their note. `gameNotFound` (404) for an unknown game. |
| `POST /api/games/:id/bookmark` `{ note? }` | Bookmarks the game, or changes the note of a bookmarked one: plain text up to 1000 characters (`BOOKMARK_NOTE_MAX`, `invalidBookmarkNote` otherwise), trimmed. Answers with `BookmarkView`. |
| `DELETE /api/games/:id/bookmark` | Removes the bookmark and its note. |
| `GET /api/studies?q=&page=` | Public studies that are not hidden, latest changed first, 30 per page (`StudyList`: `studies` and `more`), optionally whose title contains `q`. `sort=liked` lists the most liked first (ties latest changed first). With `list=mine` (signed in), every study of the player, whatever its visibility; with `list=liked` (signed in), the studies the player likes that they can still open, latest liked first. Each item carries its `likes`. |
| `POST /api/studies` `{ title, description?, visibility, topics?, chapter }` | Creates a study with its first chapter (signed in) and answers with `StudyView`. `topics` is up to `STUDY_TOPICS_MAX` of `STUDY_TOPICS`, else `invalidStudy`. `title` up to 80 characters, `description` up to 300, `visibility` `public`, `unlisted` or `private` (`invalidStudy` otherwise). A public study of an account younger than 7 days (`STUDY_PUBLIC_MIN_DAYS`) shows in its author's list (`author=`) at once and joins the public list when the account is 7 days old; until then the author, and only the author, gets that moment as `listedFrom` in the study and in their own list (`null` for everyone else, as it tells the account's age). At most 100 studies per account (`409 tooManyStudies`). `chapter` is as below. |
| `GET /api/studies?mode=&topic=&author=` | Narrows the public list (with `q`, `page` and `sort`): studies with a chapter starting in that mode, about that topic (`STUDY_TOPICS`), or by that player (an unknown name lists none). Each item carries its `topics` and the `modes` of its chapters. |
| `GET /api/studies/:id` | `StudyView`: title, description, visibility, author, whether it is hidden, the chapter list, whether the viewer is the author, how many like it (`likes`) and whether the viewer does (`liked`). A private study is `404 studyNotFound` for anyone but its author, the moderators included; a hidden one, for anyone but its author and the moderators. |
| `POST /api/studies/:id` `{ title, description?, visibility, topics? }` | The author changes the study (`403 notStudyOwner` for anyone else). Answers with `StudyView`. |
| `DELETE /api/studies/:id` | The author deletes the study with its chapters. |
| `POST /api/studies/:id/like`, `DELETE /api/studies/:id/like` | Likes a study the player can open, or takes the like back (signed in; one like per player and study, the author included). Answers with `StudyLikes`: `likes` and `liked`. A study the player cannot open is `404 studyNotFound`. |
| `GET /api/studies/:id/chapters/:chapter` | `StudyChapter`: name, start position (FEN), board orientation and the tree of moves (`root`). |
| `POST /api/studies/:id/chapters` `{ name, fen, orientation, root? }` | The author adds a chapter at the end (at most 32, `409 tooManyChapters`) and gets the `StudyChapter`. `fen` must be a position the rules can reach (`invalidChapter`). `root` is the tree: `{ comment, shapes, children }`, each child a move in the machine notation with the same fields. Every move must be legal where it is played and appear once per parent; comments take up to 1000 characters; `shapes` are arrows (`{ kind: 'arrow', from, to, color }`) and painted squares (`{ kind: 'square', square, color }`), squares numbered 0 to 80, colors `green`, `red`, `blue` or `orange`, at most 2 arrows and 4 squares per move (`invalidChapter` otherwise). A chapter has at most 500 moves and 48 KB of JSON (`chapterTooLarge`). |
| `POST /api/studies/:id/chapters/:chapter` `{ name?, orientation?, root? }` | The author renames the chapter, turns its board or saves its tree, checked as above. The editor saves the tree on its own after each change. Study writes are limited to 120 per minute per account. |
| `POST /api/studies/:id/chapters/:chapter/move` `{ index }` | The author moves the chapter to `index` in the list. Answers with `StudyView`. |
| `DELETE /api/studies/:id/chapters/:chapter` | The author deletes a chapter, never the last one (`409 lastChapter`). Answers with `StudyView`. |
| `GET /api/games/search` | `GameSearchResult`: finished games, newest first, 30 per page (`page`, up to 50), and whether there are `more`. Optional filters: `player` (either color, or the one in `color`), `vs` (their opponent), `mode`, `rated` (`1` or `0`), `result` (`white`, `black`, `draw`, or with a player `win` and `loss`), `reason`, `ratingMin` and `ratingMax` (average of both players at the start; games with an unrated player are left out), `from` and `to` (days `YYYY-MM-DD`, UTC) and `movesMin` and `movesMax` (moves, the draft's included). Anything else that does not fit is `invalidSearch`; an unknown player matches no game. Up to 30 searches (insights included) per minute per session. |
| `GET /api/games/:id` | Full game state (`GameView`). |
| `GET /api/games/:id/export` | The game as text, in the spirit of PGN: headers and one move per line, in both the rulebook and the machine notation. The analysis imports this format back (Tools > Import a game), in the browser. |
| `GET /api/tv` | Ongoing games played live (correspondence games are left out) with at least one move (`GameSummary[]`, at most 24), strongest first by the sum of both ratings. Each summary carries `clock` (milliseconds left for each side and who is running) while the game is in progress, `null` without a clock or once it has ended. |
| `GET /api/tv/channels` | The game of each TV channel (`TvChannelView[]`: `best`, then one per mode), with `game: null` when the channel has nothing to show. A channel takes the strongest game in play and keeps it until it ends and 8 seconds after, even if a stronger one starts elsewhere; then it moves to the strongest game at that moment. |
| `GET /api/lobby` | Open seeks and counters. |
| `POST /api/seeks` (`GameSettings`) | Creates a seek, or returns `{ gameId }` if it pairs immediately. A seek that has to wait needs a live WebSocket from the same session (otherwise `409 connectionRequired`), and it disappears when that connection closes or when the player starts a live game. A correspondence seek needs an account but no connection: it waits up to 7 days, survives restarts (the `seeks` table) and stays when its owner leaves or starts other games. Seeks pair only with the same pace (clock, days per move or neither). At most 3 open seeks per player, live and correspondence counted apart. |
| `POST /api/seeks/:id/accept` | Accepts a seek. |
| `DELETE /api/seeks/:id` | Cancels your own seek. |
| `POST /api/challenges` (`GameSettings`, optional `to`) | Creates a challenge link. With `to` (a username) it is a challenge from a profile: only that player can accept it, it needs a signed-in sender with a live WebSocket (`connectionRequired`) and is withdrawn when the sender's last socket closes. Refused with `userNotFound`, `cannotChallengeSelf`, `challengeRefused` (403, the player's policy) or `tooManyChallenges` (429: at most 3 pending sent per player, 10 pending received). |
| `GET /api/challenges/:id` | Challenge state. A challenge stays open for 2 hours (7 days for a correspondence game); then it becomes `canceled` (watchers get the usual `challenge` message) and is forgotten after as long again (`404 challengeNotFound`). |
| `POST /api/challenges/:id/accept` | Accepts the challenge and returns `{ gameId }`. A challenge from a profile answers `403 notYourChallenge` to anyone but its destination. |
| `POST /api/challenges/:id/decline` | The destination declines a challenge from a profile; its status becomes `declined`. |
| `DELETE /api/challenges/:id` | Cancels your own challenge. |

`GameSettings`: `{ mode, rated, timeControl: { initial, increment, byoyomi?, periods? } | null, daysPerMove, color, freeDraft }`, with `color` in `white`, `black` or `random`. A `timeControl` with `byoyomi` (10, 20, 30 or 60, `BYOYOMI_SECONDS`) is a byo-yomi clock: `periods` from 1 to 3, `increment` 0 and `initial` from 0 (`invalidByoyomi` otherwise); without it, a Fischer clock with `initial` of at least 60. Its notation is `10|30s`, or `10|3×30s` with several periods (`formatTimeControl`). With byo-yomi, `ClockView` times are the time until the flag falls, main time plus the periods left; `byoyomiState` splits them into main time, current period and periods left. White always moves first; a `firstPlayer` sent by older clients is ignored. Times in seconds. Games without a clock cannot be rated, and rated games require an account. `freeDraft` (default `false`) makes the draft end only when both players declare done; it applies only to draft modes and is not accepted in rated games. Seeks only pair with seeks that use the same draft rule.

Correspondence games: `daysPerMove` (1, 3, 7 or 14; omitted or `null` for a live game) with `timeControl: null`. Each player has that many days for every move, from the moment the opponent moved; the deadline is stored as a date, so a server restart does not extend it. Running out of time loses, except before both players have moved, when the game is aborted. Leaving is normal, so the opponent cannot claim victory, and there is no extra time button. They can be rated (same rating as live games of the mode), need an account to create and to accept (`correspondenceNeedsAccount`), and are created by challenge (from a profile or by link) or by seek in the lobby. A challenge to a player is not withdrawn when its sender leaves. Correspondence challenges and seeks are stored in the database (the `challenges` and `seeks` tables), so a restart keeps them; the account data export lists them, and deleting the account deletes them. `GameView` and `GameSummary` carry `daysPerMove` and `deadlineIn` (milliseconds the player to move had left when sent, `null` once the game is over); `GameSummary` also carries `turn`.

Errors respond with an HTTP status and `{ error, code }`: `code` is a stable code (listed in `packages/@ligungi/protocol/src/errors.ts`) for the client to translate, and `error` is a Portuguese fallback message. Each code always comes with the same status (`ERROR_STATUS` in `apps/server/src/errors.ts`).

## WebSocket `/ws`

JSON messages. The client subscribes to topics and receives updates.

Client to server:

| Message | Effect |
|---|---|
| `{ t: 'sub', topic }` / `{ t: 'unsub', topic }` | Topics: `lobby`, `tv`, `game:<id>`, `challenge:<id>`, `race:<id>`, `arena:<id>`. Being subscribed to an Arena, or to one's last Arena game, is what makes a player available for pairing. Subscribing to a game or challenge that does not exist returns an error and subscribes to nothing; a connection holds at most 32 topics (`tooManySubscriptions`). |
| `{ t: 'move', game, move, ply }` | Plays a move. `ply` is the number of moves already played. |
| `{ t: 'resign' \| 'abort' \| 'claim', game }` | Resigns, aborts or claims victory. |
| `{ t: 'draw' \| 'rematch', game, action }` | `action`: `offer`, `accept` or `decline`. |
| `{ t: 'takeback', game, action }` | Asks to take back your last move (and the opponent's reply, if they already answered), with the same actions, except that only `accept` answers the opponent's request (asking back is refused, since it would be about another move). Casual games only, never the first two moves (`canTakeback` in the game view); a pending request (`takebackOffer`) is dropped by any move. The time already spent stays spent. |
| `{ t: 'moretime', game }` | Gives the opponent 15 seconds (`EXTRA_TIME_SECONDS`), in any game with a Fischer clock (`canGiveTime`); not with byo-yomi, where the seconds would count as periods. |
| `{ t: 'queue', game, moves }` | Correspondence games, during the draft: replaces the sender's queue of placements (machine notation, up to `DRAFT_QUEUE_MAX`; `done` only as the last one, board moves refused with `invalidMove`). The server plays the next one whenever it is the sender's turn, at once if it already is, so the draft goes on while they are away; a placement that can no longer be played drops the rest of the queue. The queue empties when the draft ends, on a takeback and when the game ends, and survives a server restart. `queueUnavailable` outside the draft of a correspondence game. The game view's `draftQueue` holds the viewer's own queue, never the opponent's. |
| `{ t: 'raceMove', race, puzzle, move }` | A move in a Puzzle Racer race, on the puzzle at index `puzzle` of the race's list. The server checks it as the puzzle page does (the stored line, or in mates any other move that still forces mate) and keeps the score: a point per correct move, one more at each combo mark (5, 12, 20, 30, then every 10), and a wrong move moves on to the next puzzle. Moves for another puzzle, before the start or more than a second after the end are ignored. |
| `{ t: 'raceSkip', race, puzzle }` | Skips that puzzle, once per race, keeping the combo. |
| `{ t: 'ping' }` | Answers `pong`. |

Server to client:

| Message | When |
|---|---|
| `{ t: 'game', game }` | Any change in a subscribed game (full state). |
| `{ t: 'lobby', lobby }` | A change in the lobby. |
| `{ t: 'tv', games }` | The live games of `GET /api/tv`: on subscribing to `tv`, and again when one of them changes (a move, a new game, an end), at most twice a second. |
| `{ t: 'challenge', challenge }` | A change in a subscribed challenge. |
| `{ t: 'arena', arena }` | A change in a subscribed Arena (`ArenaView`, as seen by that player): a player joins, pauses or leaves the page, a game starts or ends, the Arena starts, finishes or is canceled. Changes are gathered and sent at most every 250 ms. When the Arena pairs a player, they also get `{ t: 'redirect', path }` to the new game. |
| `{ t: 'race', race }` | A change in a subscribed race (`RaceView`, as seen by that player): a player joins, the start is set, a score changes, the race ends. |
| `{ t: 'incoming', challenges }` | To a signed-in player, on every socket: the open challenges sent to them from profiles. Sent on connecting and after each change. |
| `{ t: 'notices', notices }` | To a signed-in player, on every socket: their latest 20 notices (`NoticeView`), newest first, each with `read`. A notice is a new follower (`follow`, with `actor`) or a moderation decision, which never names the moderator or the reason: `reportClosed` (a report they sent, `subject` is the reported player), `renamed` (`subject` is the new username), `profileCleared`, `gameVoided` (`ref` is the game), `studyHidden`, `studyRestored` (`subject` is the title, `ref` the study), `studyDeleted` and `suspensionLifted`; and the weekly Arena: `arenaSoon` (it starts in 10 minutes, for those who joined, `ref` is the Arena) and `arenaResult` (`subject` is `place/players`, or `place/players/trophy` when the place earned one). Sent on connecting and after each change. |
| `{ t: 'turns', games }` | To a signed-in player, on every socket: their correspondence games waiting for their move (`GameSummary[]`). Sent on connecting and after each change to one of their correspondence games. |
| `{ t: 'redirect', path }` | A game of yours started (rematches included). |
| `{ t: 'error', code, message }` | Action refused. After a refused move, the server resends the game state. |
