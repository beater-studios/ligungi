# Roadmap

Work the maintainer has confirmed. Candidates and ideas live in [backlog.md](backlog.md); an item moves here only once it is confirmed, and leaves this file (for the changelog) once it ships.

Size: **S** (hours to a day), **M** (a few days), **L** (a week or more).

| Item | Why | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Puzzle Racer (puzzle races against other players) | Short, competitive sessions that bring players back | M | Streak and Storm shipped; Racer races several players live on the same puzzles, so it waits for more players online. |
| Game library (curated collections of notable games) | Study material once there are good games to show | M | Collections of games from the site or imported, opened in the analysis board. |
| Studies (shared annotated analysis with chapters) | Teaching and community content | L | Builds on the analysis board, the import and the board editor. |
| Coaches directory | Connects learners with teachers | S | Profiles that opt in, with languages and contact links. |
| Videos library | Curated learning content | S | A curated list of links (YouTube and similar), filtered by language and level. |
| Streamers and broadcasts | Shows Gungi streams and events on the site | M | Only once there are Gungi streams or events to show. |
| Privacy settings: who can message or follow me | Reduces harassment | S | Who can challenge me already exists on the account page. Following can be limited now; messages once private messages exist. |
| Kid mode (no chat or messages) | Safer for young players | S | Hides chat, messages and external links. Matters most once chat or messages exist. |
| Blind mode for screen readers | Accessibility: lichess is fully playable without seeing the board, and Gungi, whose best-known player in the story is blind, should be too | L | Pages made for screen readers: the position as text (each square and its tower, tier by tier, and both hands), the squares reachable with the arrow keys and announced, moves typed and confirmed (it brings the keyboard move input of the backlog), the opponent's move, check and the end of the draft and of the game announced as they happen, clocks read on request. Moves, drops, stacking, capture and betrayal each need their own command and description. |
| Activity timeline on profiles and a feed of followed players | Social glue for the follow feature that already exists | M | What a player did each day (games, puzzles, lessons) on their profile, and the activity of followed players on the home page. |
| Recent activity feed (finished games, new players) | Shows a live site even with few players online | S | On the home page or the community page. |
| Personal insights (where you win and lose, by mode, color, phase) | Self-improvement | L | From the player's finished games: results by mode, color, game length, phase and draft choices. |
| Advanced game search | Find games by player, mode, rating, result and date across the site | M | A search page over every finished game, with filters, linked from the Tools menu. |
