# Roadmap

Work the maintainer has confirmed. Candidates and ideas live in [backlog.md](backlog.md); an item moves here only once it is confirmed, and leaves this file (for the changelog) once it ships.

Size: **S** (hours to a day), **M** (a few days), **L** (a week or more).

| Item | Why | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Byo-yomi time control (for example 10 min + 30 s), with an optional spoken countdown | The traditional clock of Japanese games: once the main time runs out, every move has a fixed period | M | New time control type in game creation, the server clock and the clock display; optionally several periods (3 x 30 s). The draft keeps its own clock rule. |
| Draft explorer | The Gungi counterpart of the lichess opening explorer; the draft is where games are won or lost | M | Statistics from finished games of the draft modes (intermediate and advanced): which pieces players place first and where, how often, and with what results, stage by stage. Filters by mode and rating range. Computed on the server from the stored games and cached, refreshed now and then. |
| Puzzles: daily and random, with ratings, themes and a personal dashboard | Daily reason to come back | L | Needs a stronger engine or a curated set. Mate problems can be the first theme. |
| Timed puzzle modes (Streak, Storm, Racer against other players) | Short, competitive sessions that bring players back | M | Built on the puzzle set, so after puzzles. |
| Practice positions against the computer | Learn key situations by playing them out | M | Positions made in the board editor, played against the bot. |
| Coordinate trainer, in the notation the player chose | Board fluency | M | Rulebook notation (column-row-tier) and the machine notation. |
| Game library (curated collections of notable games) | Study material once there are good games to show | M | Collections of games from the site or imported, opened in the analysis board. |
| Studies (shared annotated analysis with chapters) | Teaching and community content | L | Builds on the analysis board, the import and the board editor. |
| Coaches directory | Connects learners with teachers | S | Profiles that opt in, with languages and contact links. |
| Videos library | Curated learning content | S | A curated list of links (YouTube and similar), filtered by language and level. |
| Streamers and broadcasts | Shows Gungi streams and events on the site | M | Only once there are Gungi streams or events to show. |
| Privacy settings: who can message or follow me | Reduces harassment | S | Who can challenge me already exists on the account page. Following can be limited now; messages once private messages exist. |
| Kid mode (no chat or messages) | Safer for young players | S | Hides chat, messages and external links. Matters most once chat or messages exist. |
